How to Avoid UPSC Signature Rejections

  1. Use Black Ink: The UPSC scanner requires high contrast. Sign with a black ballpoint or gel pen.
  2. Crop Out Shadows: Crop tightly around the signature to remove phone camera drop-shadows and desk backgrounds.
  3. Maintain Aspect Ratio: Never stretch signatures horizontally or vertically, as distorted handwriting is flagged during verification at exam centers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the UPSC Triple Signature requirement?

For certain recent Civil Services (CSE) notices, candidates are instructed to sign three times vertically on a plain sheet with adequate spacing and scan all three into a single image between 20 KB and 100 KB.

Can I upload a signature in PNG or PDF format?

No. The UPSC portal strictly enforces .jpg or .jpeg extensions. This tool automatically converts PNGs to standardized JPG format.
