Frequently Asked Questions

Why did the SSC portal reject my photograph?

Rejections typically occur when candidates submit images outside the 20 KB to 50 KB boundary, photos with glasses/headgear, or dimensions that deviate from 3.5 cm × 4.5 cm.

Is my photo uploaded to your servers?

No. Resizing is conducted entirely in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. No images leave your phone or desktop.
