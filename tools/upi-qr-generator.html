How Does a Dynamic UPI QR Code Work?

UPI QR codes adhere to the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) deep link protocol: upi://pay?pa=VPA&pn=NAME&am=AMOUNT&cu=INR. Scanning this QR code launches any installed UPI application on the customer's smartphone with your payment details pre-filled.
