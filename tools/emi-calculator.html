The Mathematics of Equated Monthly Installments

Standard reducing balance loan EMIs are computed using the formula:

EMI = [P × r × (1 + r)n] / [(1 + r)n - 1]

Where P is the principal loan value, r is the monthly interest factor, and n represents the duration in months.
