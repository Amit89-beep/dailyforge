How Mutual Fund SIP Compounding Works

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) enables disciplined rupee-cost averaging. The formula used to calculate terminal maturity value is:

M = P × [((1 + i)n - 1) / i] × (1 + i)

Where P is your periodic deposit, i is the periodic interest rate (annual return / 12), and n is the total count of monthly instalments.
